Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026  ·  Kreeadiv LLC  ·  North Carolina, United States

We believe you deserve to understand exactly what we do with your information. This policy is written in plain language on purpose. APB is currently available to users in the United States only.

What Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the app.

If you create an account
Email address (for account creation and recovery only, never displayed publicly), username (the name you choose to show on community contributions), and password (stored encrypted we never have access to your actual password).
If you use the app without an account
We do not collect any personal information. You can browse restaurants, recipes, and content without creating an account.
If you submit content
The content of your submission (restaurant tips, product recommendations, blog post links), your name or initials if you choose to provide them, and the date and time of submission.
If you rate a restaurant or product
Your rating tied to your account and the place or product you rated.

We do not collect your real name (unless you choose to provide it), your GPS location beyond what you enter in a search, payment information, demographic information, behavioral tracking data, or data from third-party sources.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only to operate APB:

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To save your recipe book and ratings across devices
  • To display your username on content you submit
  • To review and respond to community submissions
  • To send account-related emails (password reset, account confirmation)
  • To improve the app based on how it is used

We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, use it for targeted advertising, or share it with third parties except as described below.

Your Choices and Rights
Access
You can view all information associated with your account by logging in.
Correction
You can update your username and email address in your account settings at any time.
Deletion
You can delete your account at any time from your account settings. We permanently remove your email address, username, password, saved recipes, and ratings from our system. Ratings you submitted will remain in anonymized form to preserve community data but will no longer be associated with your account or username.
Opt out of emails
You can opt out of non-essential emails by contacting us at the address below. We will always send account-related emails necessary to operate your account.
Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Passwords are encrypted and never stored in readable form. Our database provider uses industry-standard security practices. We collect the minimum information necessary to operate the app and do not store payment information.

No method of data transmission or storage is completely secure. If you have concerns about the security of your information, please contact us.

Children

APB is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

California Residents

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • The right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it (described in this policy)
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information (available in your account settings)
  • The right to opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • The right not to be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by email or through a notice in the app.
