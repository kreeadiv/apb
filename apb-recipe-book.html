Mom's Black Bean Soup
Added Mar 2 · 6 servings · 45 min · Main Plates
A rich, smoky black bean soup adapted from my mother's recipe. Oil-free and WFPB compliant I swapped the oil sauté for water-sauté and added extra cumin and smoked paprika. The secret is a splash of apple cider vinegar at the very end.
2 cans black beans
1 medium onion, diced
3 garlic cloves, minced
1 red bell pepper
2 tsp smoked paprika
1 tsp cumin
4 cups vegetable broth
1 tbsp apple cider vinegar

Rainbow Quinoa Power Bowl
Added Feb 20 · 2 servings · 20 min · Main Plates
A vibrant, nourishing bowl that comes together in 20 minutes. Fluffy quinoa topped with roasted red pepper, cucumber, shredded beet, edamame, and a lemon-tahini dressing. Fully oil-free the tahini gives all the richness you need.
1 cup cooked quinoa
½ cup shredded beet
½ cup edamame
½ cup cucumber, diced
1 roasted red pepper
2 tbsp tahini
Juice of 1 lemon
Fresh herbs to serve

Spiced Golden Milk Oatmeal
Added Jan 28 · 1 serving · 10 min · Breakfast
Creamy oats cooked in turmeric-spiced plant milk with ginger, cinnamon, and black pepper (always with turmeric!). Topped with sliced banana, a few pumpkin seeds, and a drizzle of pure maple syrup. My most-made breakfast.
½ cup rolled oats
1 cup oat milk
1 tsp ground turmeric
½ tsp ground ginger
½ tsp cinnamon
Pinch black pepper
1 banana, sliced
1 tsp maple syrup
